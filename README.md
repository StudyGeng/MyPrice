# MyPrice

MyPrice helps shoppers check item prices and keep track of a shopping budget. Store owners can add their items and share a QR code for shoppers to scan.

**App status:** MyPrice is still being tested on an Android phone. There is no public app download on GitHub yet.

## Shop with MyPrice

You do not need an account. After MyPrice is installed:

1. Tap **Let's Shop** and scan a participating store's MyPrice QR code. You can also enter the store code manually.
2. Choose **Member** or **Non-Member**, set your budget, and tap **Next**.
3. Scan an item's barcode to see its price and any promotion. Add the item to your cart.
4. In **Cart**, check your total and remaining budget. Tap **Scan** to add more items or **Checkout** to finish.
5. Use the history icon on the home screen to review past shopping sessions saved on your phone.

**Checkout records a shopping summary; it does not take payment.** You need an internet connection to start a new shopping session and load store items.

## Use MyPrice as a store owner

1. Tap the store icon on the home screen, then **Register Now**.
2. Enter your store name, location, email address, and password. The approval reference field is optional.
3. Open the verification link sent to your email. Wait for the MyPrice administrator to approve your store, then sign in.
4. Tap **+** to add an item's barcode, name, and price. Promotions are optional. Tap an existing item to update or remove it.
5. Open **My Account** and tap **Generate Unique QR Code**. Show the code to shoppers, or save a screenshot to print it.

The new app starts without stores or items. Shoppers can scan your store after your account is approved, you sign in, and you add items. If you forget your password, use **Forget password** on the login screen.
